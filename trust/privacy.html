Privacy Policy

Draft updated 12 September 2026

What WORKIT processes

WORKIT is a video-first professional identity, hiring and marketplace platform. Depending on how you use the service, we may process account information, professional profile data, videos and project content, job applications, marketplace activity, messages, reports, device information and transaction records.

Why we use data

  • Operate accounts, profiles, search and discovery.
  • Enable hiring, applications, messaging, services, products and orders.
  • Protect users, investigate reports and prevent abuse or fraud.
  • Measure product performance and improve relevance.
  • Meet legal, accounting and security obligations.

Your choices

You can edit professional information, control notification categories, request account deletion and contact WORKIT regarding privacy requests. Data that must be retained by law or for legitimate dispute/accounting purposes may be kept only for the required period.

Service providers

WORKIT may use infrastructure, authentication, video hosting, storage, notification, analytics and payment providers. Production disclosure must list the providers actually enabled at launch.

International use

WORKIT is intended for a global audience. Production legal review must define the controller entity, lawful bases, international transfer mechanisms, retention periods and jurisdiction-specific rights, including applicable European data-protection rights.

Contact

A production privacy contact address will be published before launch.
