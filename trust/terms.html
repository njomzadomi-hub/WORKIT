Using WORKIT

WORKIT helps people and organizations show real work, discover professionals, find jobs, recruit, communicate and transact. Users are responsible for keeping account information accurate and for content, offers, jobs and communications they publish.

Professional and employer content

Do not misrepresent qualifications, work history, prices, job opportunities, company identity or project results. Content that is fraudulent, unsafe, unlawful, abusive or infringes others' rights may be removed and accounts may be restricted.

Jobs and marketplace activity

WORKIT provides technology that connects users. Production terms must define the platform's role in contracts between users, marketplace fees, payment processing, cancellations, refunds, disputes, taxes and seller/employer obligations before payments are enabled.

Safety and moderation

WORKIT may review reports and take proportionate action including limiting reach, removing content, suspending transactions or restricting accounts when necessary to protect the platform or comply with law.

Account deletion

Users may initiate account deletion from Account settings. WORKIT will delete associated personal data that is not required to be retained for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or dispute purposes.

Final production terms

Before launch this draft must be completed with the legal entity, governing law, dispute terms, age requirements, intellectual-property rules, liability limits, prohibited conduct, payment terms and jurisdiction-specific consumer rights.
