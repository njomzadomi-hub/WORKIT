Delete your WORKIT account

You can initiate deletion inside WORKIT at Profile → Settings → Account, or use the verified web flow below when it is activated for launch.

When a deletion request is accepted, your public profile is hidden immediately. WORKIT then permanently deletes your authentication account and personal profile/content, except records that must be retained and anonymized for applicable legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or active-dispute obligations.

Verified web deletion request

Sign in with the WORKIT account you want to delete. Your password is sent directly to Supabase Auth for verification and is not stored by this page.








What may be retained

Transaction records required for legitimate legal/accounting obligations may be retained only in an anonymized form for the required period. Production policy will state the final retention rules.
