Delete your WORKIT account

The fastest way to initiate deletion is inside the WORKIT app:

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Account.
  4. Choose Delete account and confirm.

When a request is initiated, the public profile is hidden immediately. WORKIT then completes permanent deletion of account data that is not required to be retained for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or active-dispute purposes.

Request deletion outside the app

Before public launch, this page will include a verified request form so users who no longer have app access can initiate account deletion from the web.

What may be retained

Only records that must be preserved for applicable legal, accounting, security or dispute obligations may be retained for the required period. Production policy will state the specific retention rules.
